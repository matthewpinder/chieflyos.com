Leave

The right to leave.

You can take everything with you. You can uninstall Chiefly. You do not owe us a reason, a note, or a goodbye.

Take your things

  1. Open Chiefly.

    It is the app on your computer.

  2. Export everything.

    The button for this is TBD. The promise is one action. It writes a copy of what Chiefly holds onto your computer, in a file you keep.

  3. Put that file somewhere you will find it.

    It is yours. Uninstalling the app does not delete that file.

If you never opted into the cloud, we do not hold a copy of the work. If you did opt in, email [contact email] and ask us to delete what we hold.

Uninstall on a Mac

  1. Quit Chiefly.

    The app should be closed.

  2. Open your Applications folder.

    Find Chiefly.

  3. Drag Chiefly to the Trash.

    Empty the Trash when you are ready. The app is gone.

Uninstall on a Windows PC

  1. Quit Chiefly.

    The app should be closed.

  2. Open Settings, then Apps.

    Find Chiefly in the list.

  3. Click Uninstall.

    Follow the short prompts. They belong to Windows. When they finish, the app is gone.

What Chiefly stored only on that computer goes with the app. Your export file stays.

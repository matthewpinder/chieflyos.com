Vision

Where Chiefly is going.

The direction is a handful of outcomes. This is the list.

  1. A tool, not a god.

    It does the chores. You decide.

  2. You own it.

    One purchase. You do not rent it. Your copy keeps working, even if we don’t.

  3. Local first.

    It runs on your computer. Cloud only if you opt in.

  4. A notebook you can burn.

    Memory you can open. Pages you can tear out. See it, and delete it, anytime.

  5. It learns from the work.

    Not from a questionnaire. From what you do together.

  6. The right to leave.

    Export everything. Uninstall. Nothing begs you to stay. The right to leave.

  7. It outlives the company.

    No central switch exists to seize it. The copy on your computer is the one that remains.

The day, later

  • The day starts clear.
  • It asks before it acts.
  • You can see what it remembers.
  • You can leave with everything.

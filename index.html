Questions.

Is Chiefly private?

Chiefly is a private AI that runs on your computer. Local by default, cloud opt-in. You can see and delete what it remembers, anytime.

Is Chiefly a subscription?

No. Chiefly is a one-time payment, never a subscription. You pay once, and your Chiefly keeps working, even if we don’t. Updates may stop; your copy keeps working.

What is founder’s pricing?

Early access is Regular $199, and Student $69, which covers four years or until graduation. At launch it is Regular $299, and Student $99. The first 100 buyers are founders. Launch prices won’t go back down. Early access isn’t open yet. Join the waitlist to get founder’s pricing.

Does Chiefly work offline?

Yes, with a local model. A local model runs on your computer, so it can work without a network. Cloud is opt-in, and that part needs a connection.

Does Chiefly run on Mac and Windows?

Chiefly is coming to Mac and Windows.

Privacy Policy

Receipts.

Your AI works for you. Theirs reports to a cloud. Everything below is a receipt, not a promise.

  • Private stays private.

    Private, school, and personal work never leaves your computer. Not to another AI, not in a delegation, and not by an accident of wording. A private turn shuts every outside door.

  • Only the public task goes.

    When you approve a delegation, a filter checks that no private note name, file path, body, email, or phone number rides along. Only the public task goes.

  • Private turns reach nothing.

    A socket-level proof shows a private turn makes zero outside calls. A normal public turn can be caught reaching the weather service. Private turns reach nothing.

  • What left this computer.

    A panel shows every real send and every refusal, from the actual logs. It never invents a number. Anything it does not track says “not tracked.”

  • Your files stay yours.

    Every file Chiefly saves is locked to its owner.

  • The PIN guards approvals.

    Sensitive approvals happen on an in-page PIN pad. You see dots only, and the browser is never offered the PIN to save. An approval cannot skip the PIN.

  • Signed updates.

    Chiefly only installs an update it can verify.

  • It can’t fake a done.

    The completion record is written from what the tools actually did. If a step was held, refused, or failed, the record says “Not done” and names the step.

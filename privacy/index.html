Privacy

What this site keeps.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This website and the waitlist

This covers chieflyos.com and the waitlist on it. The Chiefly app is a separate section below. Joining the list is not the same thing as using the app.

What we collect

When you join, we store your email address. We store which form you used: the top of the home page, the bottom of the home page, the paper page, the rename page, or the vision page.

When the page address includes them, we store the campaign tags utm_source, utm_campaign, and utm_content. We also store utm_medium when it is there. Each tag is cut at 200 characters. A blank tag is left off. The paper page and the rename page set utm_content to that page, so a different content tag in the link does not replace it.

We store the site that linked you here. That is the origin and the path, cut at 500 characters. The query string is not stored.

A computer choice is optional. If you tell us Mac, Windows, or both, we store that as platform. If you skip it, we store nothing for it.

There is an optional box, Build with me. If you tick it, we store build_with_me as true. That means you want to vote on what is next and join early tests. If you leave it unticked, we store nothing for it.

Where it is stored, and why

The row is stored in Supabase. We keep it so we can tell you about Chiefly. We will only email you about Chiefly.

We do not sell this list. We do not share it for advertising.

How to be removed

Email matt@chieflyos.com and ask to be removed. We will delete your waitlist row.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set cookies. It does not use analytics. The scripts do not load a tracker. The only request they send is the waitlist row, to Supabase.

On your own browser, the site remembers that you already joined, in local storage, so the form can show that. For this visit it also remembers that the start-up screen has played, in session storage. Those stay on your device. They are not cookies, and they are not sent to us.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary logs of requests to the site, such as the page, the time, and an IP address. Those logs are GitHub’s, under GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not keep a separate analytics log.

The Chiefly app

The app is not this website. It is local by default. It runs on your computer. Cloud only if you opt in.

Joining the waitlist does not opt you into the cloud, and it does not upload the work the app holds. What it remembers on your computer stays there unless you opt in. You can see and delete what it remembers.

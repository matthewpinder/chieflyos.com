Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Chiefly is a personal AI chief of staff that runs on your own computer. This policy covers this website (chieflyos.com) and, briefly, how the Chiefly app itself handles your information. It is written in plain words on purpose. Chiefly is run by Matt Pinder, its sole owner.

The website

What we collect. If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you enter. We store which form you used (the top or bottom of the home page, the paper page, or the vision page). A computer choice is optional: if you pick Mac, Windows, or both, we store that; if you skip it, we store nothing for it. If you tick the “Build with me” box, we record that you want to vote on what’s next and join early tests; if you leave it unticked, we store nothing for it.

When the page address includes them, we also store the campaign tags utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, and utm_content. Each tag is cut at 200 characters, and a blank tag is left off. The paper and vision pages set utm_content to that page, so a different content tag in the link does not replace it. We store the site that linked you here (the origin and the path, cut at 500 characters). The query string is not stored.

That is all this website collects. There is no name field.

Where it is stored. Waitlist entries are stored in a database hosted by Supabase, a database provider. Access to that database is restricted; the public form can add an entry and cannot read the list.

What we use it for. To tell you when Chiefly opens, and — only if you ticked “Build with me” — to invite you to early tests and founder votes. Nothing else. We never sell your information, and we never share it for anyone else’s marketing.

Cookies and analytics. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. The scripts do not load a tracker. The only request they send off this site is the waitlist row, to Supabase, when you join.

On your own browser, the site remembers that you already joined, in local storage, so the form can show that. For this visit it also remembers that the start-up screen has played, in session storage. Those stay on your device. They are not cookies, and they are not sent to us.

Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary logs of requests to the site, such as the page, the time, and an IP address. Those logs are GitHub’s, under GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not keep a separate analytics log.

Email. We do not use a separate email service for the waitlist today. When we email you about Chiefly, the message will include a way to unsubscribe. You can also write to matt@chieflyos.com and we will remove you.

Your choices. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to matt@chieflyos.com. Deleting removes your waitlist entry completely.

Children. Chiefly is not made for children, and this website is not directed at them. If you believe a child has joined the waitlist, contact us and we will delete the entry.

The Chiefly app

Your work stays on your computer. Chiefly runs locally. What you ask it, what it remembers, and the files it works with stay on your machine. The app sends us nothing: no telemetry, no usage analytics, no crash reports, and there is no account with us. If you file a problem report in the app, it is saved on your computer only unless you choose to send it yourself.

Optional cloud use. Local is the default. If you choose to connect a cloud AI service, you do that with your own key or settings on your computer, not by signing into an account with us. Some cloud steps, such as handing a task to an online helper, wait for your approval and show you what would leave. Other optional settings you turn on can send a question you just asked to the service you connected. Private work you mark as private, school work, and personal details are not sent.

Updates. Chiefly only installs an update it can verify as signed. An update it cannot verify is refused.

Payments. When you buy Chiefly, a payment processor handles your card. We never see or store your card number.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects the waitlist, we will email the list before it takes effect.

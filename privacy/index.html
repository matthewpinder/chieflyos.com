Privacy

Local by default

Chiefly runs on your computer. That is the start. Cloud only if you opt in.

If you do not opt in, the work stays on your computer. We do not receive it.

What opt-in sends

Opt-in sends the piece of work you chose to send, so a cloud model can do that task. It leaves your computer because that is what a cloud model is.

We do not sell it

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising.

This website

The waitlist stores your email, which form you used, and the campaign tags on the link when they are there: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, and utm_content. It also stores the site that linked you here, as the origin and path only. The row is kept in Supabase, so we can tell you about Chiefly.

This site does not set cookies. It does not use analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary logs of requests.

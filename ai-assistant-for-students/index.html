For students

An AI assistant for students.

Chiefly for students is a one-time AI. You pay once. The student price covers four years, or until graduation. It is not a subscription that bills you each term.

This page will not guess what a typical student already pays for software, and it will not invent a stack of other bills. The fact Chiefly can state is smaller. The student price is one payment. Early access is Student $69. At launch it is Student $99. Regular early access is $199, and regular at launch is $299, so the student price sits next to the full price in the open. The first 100 buyers are founders. Launch prices won’t go back down. This is the price. Early access is not open yet, so there is no buy button.

What it is for

What you get is a personal AI chief of staff on your computer. It remembers what matters and keeps your goals in sight. For a student, that can be the work you said you cared about, the deadline you do not want to lose, and the shape of the week. It handles boring tracking so the day stays readable.

It does not promise grades. It does not do the learning for you. A tool that wrote your education would be a different product, and a worse one. Chiefly is the chief of staff. You are the student. The boring stuff is the tracking and the remembering. The thinking stays yours.

It asks before it acts. Nothing happens until you say so. It will not send, change, or spend on its own. A spend waits for your PIN. If a proposal is wrong, you refuse it, and nothing is sent. All clear. That is the right default when an assistant sits next to your name. You stay the person who acts. The rule is spelled out on It asks before it acts.

On your computer, including offline

It runs on your computer. Local by default, cloud opt-in. That is the same privacy claim as the rest of Chiefly, and it matters in a dorm, on a bus, or on a library network that drops. With a local model, Chiefly can work offline. If you opt into the cloud, that part needs a network. You choose. The private AI assistant page is the longer version of where the work lives.

You can see and delete what it remembers, anytime. Notes about your week should not be stuck in a place you cannot open. Seeing them, and deleting them, is part of owning the copy. A semester is long enough that this should be ordinary, not a special request.

One payment, then the copy is yours

You pay once, and your Chiefly keeps working, even if we don’t. Updates may stop; your copy keeps working. The copy on your computer does not need our servers to remain yours. If you opted into cloud features, those need a service. The local copy keeps working either way.

Four years, or until graduation, is a long time to keep a seat warm with a monthly bill. The student price is one payment meant to cover that span. When the span ends, you are not halfway through a plan that only makes sense if you keep paying. You already paid. The copy is on your machine.

No nudges unless you set them. School is already full of pings. Chiefly does not add a stream of them. You can set a nudge if you want one. You are not opted into interruptions. An approval is not a nudge. An approval appears because an action is waiting, and the action will not happen alone.

Machines, and the name

Chiefly is coming to Mac and Windows. Bring the computer you already use for school. There is no separate student appliance, and there is no claim here about a model of laptop. If it is a Mac or a Windows PC, that is the pair Chiefly is coming to.
